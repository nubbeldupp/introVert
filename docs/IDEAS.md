# introVert idea backlog

Ideas parked for later. Built ones move to the README.

## Social shields

- **Whisper inbox**: whispers land silently in an "Inbox" window tab (no
  sound, no flashing chat tab); read and reply when you feel like it.
- **Calm cities**: in capital cities hide other players' nameplates and pets,
  restore your settings when you leave.

## Solo progression

- **Mythic dungeon mounts**: Mythic-only drops (Sharkbait from Freehold,
  Mummified Raptor Skull from Kings' Rest, Return to Karazhan, Mechagon,
  Necrotic Wake, ...). These have a weekly lockout, so lockout recording
  would need to include dungeons first. Also the old Magisters' Terrace
  hawkstrider once it is clear how Midnight's remade dungeon handles it.
- **Rare alert**: quiet notice when a rare with a mount drop shows on the
  map (vignettes); needs a curated rare list.
