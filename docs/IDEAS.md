# introVert idea backlog

Ideas parked for later. Built ones move to the README.

## Social shields

- **Whisper inbox**: whispers land silently in an "Inbox" window tab (no
  sound, no flashing chat tab); read and reply when you feel like it.
- **Calm cities**: in capital cities hide other players' nameplates and pets,
  restore your settings when you leave.

## Solo progression

- **Dungeon mounts**: old dungeon drops (Rivendare's Deathcharger, Sethekk
  Raven Lord, Blue Proto-Drake, ...) in the Mounts tab with attempts; same
  data model as raids, verified with `/iv mounts check`.
- **Finder checklist**: the Finder plan becomes a checklist that ticks itself
  off (mount boss killed, delve finished) during the session.
- **Session journal**: "Journal" tab logging each session (raid kills,
  delves, Labyrinth chambers, achievements, attempts); one line on next
  login: "Last session: 3 raids, 2 delves, 1 achievement".
- **Rare alert**: quiet notice when a rare with a mount drop shows on the
  map (vignettes); needs a curated rare list.

## Comfort

- **German translation** of all texts.
