# introVert

**The quiet side of Azeroth.** Mutes group chatter, declines invites and spam, and plans your solo week: raid mounts, delves, Great Vault and alts.

![introVert logo](SCREENSHOT_LOGO)

For players who like to play alone, or in groups without the small talk. No guild pings, no duel spam, no "gogogo" in LFR. Just you and your goals.

---

## 🤫 Quiet Groups

Random dungeons and LFR, without the noise.

- **Chat filter** per profile (dungeon / LFR): show all, only keywords, only the leader, or nothing
- **Auto greet and farewell**: one polite line, then silence
- **Leave button** when the run is done
- **Ready alarm** so you never miss a ready check while tabbed out
- **Encounter focus**: chat muted during boss fights and for a short time after a wipe
- Hidden lines are never lost: `/iv quiet log`

![Quiet Groups in LFR](SCREENSHOT_QUIET)

## 🚫 Do Not Disturb

- Auto-decline **guild invites**, **duels**, **pet battle duels** and **group invites** (friends still get through)
- **Recruitment spam filter** for channels, whispers, say and yell (English and German)
- Optional polite whisper reply: *"Thanks, but I play solo!"*
- **Away reply**: while you are AFK, everyone who whispers gets one friendly reply, and you see who whispered when you are back
- See what was blocked: `/iv dnd log`

![Do Not Disturb log](SCREENSHOT_DND)

## 🐉 Raid Farm Planner

- **Lockouts across all your characters**: who is free for which raid this week
- **Missing raid mounts** with boss, difficulty and **attempt counter**
- **Drop luck**: "1% drop: 33% of players would have it after 40 attempts"
- Shows which alts can still try for each mount

![Raids tab](SCREENSHOT_RAIDS)
![Mounts tab](SCREENSHOT_MOUNTS)

## 🕯️ Delve Companion

- **Great Vault** world progress at a glance
- Today's **bountiful delves**
- Your **delve history** with tiers
- **Labyrinth of Kindo'jan** support: chambers per visit and best tier

![Delves tab](SCREENSHOT_DELVES)

## 🏆 Solo Achievement Hunter

Finds achievements you can actually finish alone, closest to completion first. PvP, Feats of Strength and Legacy are skipped; group content is tagged.

![Achievements tab](SCREENSHOT_ACHIEVEMENTS)

## 🧭 Solo Content Finder

Got an hour? `/iv finder 60` builds a plan of the best-value solo activities that fit your time, with vault-relevant delves marked.

![Finder tab](SCREENSHOT_FINDER)

## 👥 Alts

Every character at a glance: level, race and class, item level, Great Vault progress, rewards waiting, gold, and total gold across the account.

![Alts tab](SCREENSHOT_ALTS)

## Getting started

- Click the **minimap button** (left-click: window, right-click: options)
- Or type `/iv` for all commands
- All settings: **Options → AddOns → introVert** (`/iv options`)

![Options panel](SCREENSHOT_OPTIONS)

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/iv` | List all commands |
| `/iv window` | Open the introVert window |
| `/iv options` | Settings |
| `/iv quiet on\|off` | Quiet Groups on or off |
| `/iv dnd on\|off` | Do Not Disturb on or off |
| `/iv raids [name]` | Lockouts, or who is free for one raid |
| `/iv mounts` | Missing raid mounts and attempts |
| `/iv delves` | Delve Companion |
| `/iv ach` | Solo achievements |
| `/iv finder [minutes]` | Plan your session |
| `/iv alts` | All characters |
| `/iv minimap` | Show or hide the minimap button |
